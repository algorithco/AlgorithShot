# Code of conduct

AlgorithShot is built by Algorithco and an open community of contributors.
We want everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, reviews and
other project spaces to feel welcome and able to do their best work, whatever
their background, identity or level of experience.

## Our standards

Please:

- be kind and patient, and assume good intent;
- give feedback about the work, not the person, and explain your reasons;
- accept feedback gracefully, and own and fix your mistakes;
- respect people's time, privacy, opinions and experience.

Do not:

- harass, insult or demean anyone, in public or in private;
- use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances;
- make discriminatory jokes or comments, or personal or political attacks;
- publish anyone's private information, such as an address or a private
  screenshot, without their explicit permission;
- keep contacting someone who has asked you to stop.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space. It also applies when someone
represents the project in public, for example through an official account or
at an event.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@algorithco.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially and fairly. A maintainer who is involved in a report takes no
part in handling it.

## Enforcement

Depending on what happened, the maintainers may:

1. explain privately what was wrong and ask for a change;
2. give a formal warning;
3. remove or edit comments, commits and other contributions;
4. ban someone from the project, for a time or permanently.

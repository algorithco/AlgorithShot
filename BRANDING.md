# Names and logos

AlgorithShot is developed by Algorithco and its contributors. The source code,
including the logo artwork in this repository, is available under the
[MIT License](LICENSE). These guidelines describe how to identify your work
without implying endorsement by Algorithco.

You may use the project name to describe the app, link to it, credit it, or
explain that your project is based on it. Keep copyright and license notices
when redistributing the code.

For a modified build or fork, clearly identify your changes and publisher in
the README, release notes and download page. A distinct app name, identifier
and icon help users distinguish your build from official releases and avoid
sharing application data or installer registrations with the official app.
Use your own update endpoint and signing key if you distribute updates.

Do not present your fork or downloads as an official Algorithco release or
claim sponsorship or endorsement without permission. These guidelines do not
add restrictions to the MIT software license. Questions about branding or
endorsement can be sent to **hello@algorithco.com**.
